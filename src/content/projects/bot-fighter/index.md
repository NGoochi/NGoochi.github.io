Two armed cars, one oversized ball, one arena. Before the match you spend a budget on chassis upgrades and weapons. During it you score by putting the ball in the other goal (3 points) or by destroying the other car (1 point).

It started from a written design brief with four pillars, in priority order: handling first, builds that create matchups, the ball as a weapon too, and 60 fps in a browser tab on integrated graphics.

- **Garage:** four legal build presets, five chassis upgrades, hardpoints for front, turret and side weapons, three save slots, and full cosmetic customisation.
- **Five drivers** with generated 90s-comic portraits and 3D figures, bios, taunt lines and an ultimate each: Stampede, Overclock, Shadow Clone, Triple Play and Slow Jam.
- **Arcade handling** on a 120 Hz Rapier physics tick: boost, jump, dodge, air control, power-slide, and wall riding.
- **Solo against a bot** at three difficulties, or local split-screen. Works with keyboard or a gamepad.

The live build is served by GitHub Pages from a deploy-only repo. The source isn't public.
